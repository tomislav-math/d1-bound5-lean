Outcome

D1_bound5-v2.tex. The main theorem is unchanged: every D(1)-tuple in the full

checks actually performed; it is not a claim of proof-assistant certification.

Principal changes

1. The fourth-entry determinant estimate now proves T > 3ABS when A >= 3.
   This gives the simple constant K = 400 in Section 6 and removes its former
   phase maximization argument. The two new determinant endpoints and the
   precise rational coefficient exceeding 400 have been checked exactly.

2. Algebraic integrality proves full rank of the four-row P construction
   throughout its existing domain. The former six-row P0 alternative and its
   extra branches disappear. Section 6 now needs 15 endpoint comparisons.
   The combined H and V coefficients are defined before absolute values are
   taken. Their residual polynomials give the explicit sufficient bound 3.
   Thus the old unexplained constants 145 and 300 do not survive here.

3. An explicit quadratic inequality for irregular triples replaces the final
   fourth-entry search. Of the 56 nonrational rows, 38 are excluded directly by
   the tail bound, 16 by exact outward-enclosure endpoint inequalities, and
   the last two by the quadratic and a short modulo-16 norm argument. The
   largest of those 16 upper bounds is exactly
   -526504132441877163783/5000000000000000000 < -100.
   All 56 M_bs and N0 entries are retained. Only the obsolete search-output
   column is deleted. The rational triple (1,3,8) is still excluded internally
   by N0 = 1024 and the incompatible bounds t < 32 and t > 48.

4. The relevant earlier recommendations are accounted for in the change
   register and the literature/editorial ledger. They include the branch-cut
   wording, explicit logarithmic weakening, naming all six entries, parity
   cases for the full integer ring, local notation fixes, shorter headings,
   unused equation numbers, punctuation, obsolete assembly comments and
   precise citation locators. Optional global basis harmonization N04 was not
   recommended and was not applied. Superseded recommendations were resolved
   against the new proof rather than reintroducing deleted text.

5. The historical subring results are distinguished from the present full-ring
   argument. Franušić–Kreso is cited under its published author order, title,
   journal volume and year; the preprint DOI is clearly identified separately.
   Conflicting page ranges were not guessed. He–Togbé–Ziegler is not needed.

Source binding and reproducibility

Each mathematical result records a source hash. The expensive Section 7 runs
were completed on the initial v2 hash 66f5a580...587c63, before four final
editorial corrections elsewhere. The exact four replacements are reversed in
memory to recover that full source hash, and the entire Section 7 is verified
byte-identical to the final source. The final cheap checks were rerun. The
original run hashes and runtimes are retained honestly; they are not relabelled
as executions on a later file. The supplied graph program now accepts the final
source, so a reader can rerun everything directly.

The verification scripts use Python's standard library and integer/Fraction
arithmetic for mathematical decisions. Their logs and row-level JSON results
are included. The README gives integrity, quick, full and regeneration commands.
The aggregate integrity check verifies these artifacts and source relationships;
it does not itself constitute a fresh execution of the mathematical algorithms.

Limits

The all-order Bennett determinant identity remains an explicit published input.
Its original cited passage and the manuscript's specialization/extension were
reviewed; no new elementary proof for arbitrary order is claimed. The proof
still uses the other finite enumerations and interval certificates described in
the manuscript. Removing the final fourth-entry search does not make the entire
paper computation-free. Independent implementations can share conceptual
assumptions, so their agreement is evidence, not a formal guarantee.

For Franušić–Kreso, the available author records disagree on pagination. The
manuscript omits the uncertain pages. The literature report distinguishes full
passages actually inspected from abstract/metadata-only access. No exhaustive
priority or novelty certification is asserted.
