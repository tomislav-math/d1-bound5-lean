## Checks of the substantive v2 changes and their consumers

1. **Removing the six-row P0 construction is justified.** The new proof uses E as an algebraic integer, an arithmetic fact available in the paper's original setting. There is no circular dependence on any later interval or structural growth estimate. C≥200S alone is enough for the nonzero-E contradiction. The other finite-form guards remain necessary for the coefficient and residual estimates and have been retained.
2. **No stale rank restriction survives in Sections 4–5.** The P statement now holds for every q≥1 with B≥qA. Whole-source searches find no `ff-P0`, `ff-p0`, `ff-allSrank`, old coefficient 39000 or old residual 45961. 3. **The all-large consumer uses the right weaker constant.** Source lines 1909–1910 select P with residual 3. Its upper endpoint at line 1924 is A⁶C⁹/(4·3²S⁴), and the P→N3/P→A3 overlap denominators at lines 1948–1949 use 16·3². The obsolete S≥2B/P0 branching is absent. The remaining branch conditions involving S versus B² or B³ concern overlap algebra, not rank, and are legitimate distinct conditions. This is a dependency check, not a replacement for the assigned complete interval-chain audit.
4. **Small-entry consumers remain valid.** Section 8 uses the same adaptive residual and both lower-endpoint terms. Section 9 uses q=[b0/a0]− and 2400, now without needing the deleted rank restriction. The distance-sensitive Section 8/9 refinements remain present; removing the Section 6 phase detour does not remove their definitions.
5. **The contour wording has been repaired without changing the estimate.** The new sentence states the modulus of the difference of boundary values, not a difference of their moduli. Direct exact checks of the two phases for k=1,…,5 and the general half-integer formula agree.
6. **The H/V explanation preserves the smaller correct W values.** In particular W_H(1)=1770 and W_V(1)=5268 are now obtained by an unambiguous procedure. Taking the component rows' absolute coefficients before combination would give larger totals and is not what v2 says.

## Cleared objections and limits

- Equal moduli do not invalidate rank: entry distinctness, rather than strict modulus ordering, supplies all difference factors. The E argument is also valid at equal moduli.
- A zero auxiliary tuple root is not divided out in the root-transfer proofs. The only denominators involving a tuple root are z or Z, whose nonvanishing is separately established.
- The max over rows in the residual does not require one row to maximize both residual and coefficient estimates: a full-rank matrix supplies some nonzero row, and the uniform bounds apply to that row.
- Finite determinant probes for the ordinary construction do not prove the all-m,k identity. Its proof uses the freshly inspected Bennett identity, the displayed clearing calculation and polynomial continuation. The new finite-family determinants and P/Q determinants, by contrast, were fully expanded as polynomials.
