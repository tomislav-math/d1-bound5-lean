P02. Strengthen the fourth-entry estimate and simplify Section 6

Location: Lemma 2.5 and its proof, lines 387–509; Proposition 6.1 proof,
lines 1910–2083.
Status: Recommended, proved mathematical improvement. The present proof is valid.
Issue: Section 6 uses T>2ABS and retains an additional estimate involving
\mathcal Q^2\Phi to pass one interval comparison. The same determinant
calculation already gives T>3ABS when A>=3. The resulting stronger common
gap makes that extra estimate unnecessary.
Reason: This removes a maximization argument and several local constants
without introducing a new construction, search, or named result. Improving the
auxiliary constant is useful here because it shortens the proof of the main
theorem. It does not improve the bound of five itself.

Proof of the strengthening:
The determinant estimate in Lemma 2.5 requires AB>5, not A<3. The latter
condition is used only to choose the lower endpoint 2/3 in its last step.
For A>=3, Lemma 2.3 already gives tau=T/(ABS)>2. In the same positive
majorant take alpha=B_0=3, so k_1=k_2=65/126. Exact substitution gives

  F(2) = 27298839409/40831674912 < 7/10,
  F(3) = 57530072833/61247512368 < 19/20.

The coefficient of 1/tau is positive. Convexity excludes 2<tau<=3 because
then F(tau)<1, contrary to the nonzero integral determinant. Thus T>3ABS.
The existing weaker Lemma 2.3 remains the bootstrap, so the argument is not
circular. The existing five small-first-entry cases of Lemma 2.5 are retained.

With c_*=(9999/10000)(200/19)^2 and mu=51051/400000, the retained gap gives

  C > 3^5 c_* (1/8-mu/81)^2 A^7 B^5 S^3
    = (16405041495117/40000000000) A^7 B^5 S^3
    > 400 A^7 B^5 S^3.

Use K=400 and the uniform residual R_bs=35/32. At the previously exceptional
N_{3,bs} to A_4 comparison the sufficient quotient becomes

  K^2 3^14 / (16 R_bs^2 M_4^2 h)
    = 1224133953984/844113475625 > 7/5,

where M_4=166000 and h=4001/3999. All eighteen Section 6 comparisons
were checked exactly with the new constant. This is their smallest value.
The ordinary infinite-tail comparison also passes.

WHAT SHOULD REMAIN

The title and current abstract accurately identify the main theorem. An
expanded abstract spelling out D(1) is possible, but the present concise
abstract is satisfactory and no replacement is recommended. Delta is already
defined globally, so a second definition in Lemma 8.2 is unnecessary. The valid
Delta<=64 enumeration cutoff in the proof of Lemma 2.2 should also be retained. No global
replacement of “anchor”, “regular triple” or useful table-family symbols is
recommended. A large table is not redundant merely because its entries can
also be regenerated from code. The finite search outputs remain in the article.

Keep the integral r_0 hypothesis in Lemma 7.5. Every application satisfies it.
Keep the allowances for zero square values, equal moduli and half-integral
full-ring coordinates. Keep the distinction between integral tuple roots and
auxiliary analytic square roots. The proof does not assume unique factorization.
No p-adic continued-fraction algorithm is used, so the prompt's cautions about
Schneider/Ruban/Browkin algorithms do not generate an issue in this manuscript.
