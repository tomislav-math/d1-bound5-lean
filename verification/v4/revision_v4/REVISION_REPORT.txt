Mathematical changes

1. The f_3 estimate now has an explicit short proof using
   (1-w)^2(1+w)=(1-w)(1-w^2). This gives the actual constants
   45/1024 and 85/1024 without a trigonometric optimization.
2. The product-four estimate is strengthened from (3/8)ABC to (3/2)ABC.
   Its proof uses the already available D>6C and gives the exact upper
   coefficient 18212679025/27518828544<2/3. Later estimates do not rely
   on exploiting the improvement.
3. The four-point Vandermonde bound is proved by its 24 monomials, removing
   the maximum-modulus/Hadamard step. The changed local constants are 13
   and 49/50. Both downstream consumers were recalculated; their final
   gap and cutoff conclusions, and all printed N_0 values, remain valid.
4. The retained-gap exponent is A^4 as obtained by the derivation. The
   small-cutoff estimate explicitly uses AT>=T>=10^72>11. The Legendre
   application explicitly includes u/v+sqrt(d)>4.
5. Bennett's identity is identified precisely as the unnumbered identity
   after (2.4), Section 2, page 1721, with the normalization and extra pole
   explained. The extension keeps the hypothesis alpha_0=0. The all-orders
   identity remains a cited result; finite tests are not presented as its
   proof.
6. The elementary root-transfer proof appears once and is reused. Its
   nonzero denominators and possible zero root values are treated explicitly.
   The expression for R_2 is simplified by cancelling A.

Scope and limitations

This is a mathematical/computational review, not a proof-assistant
formalization. The all-orders Bennett identity is supported by its cited
published source, not by finite sampling. The large extension graphs through
modulus 10^550 and the Pell extension graphs were NOT regenerated in this
v4 run. Their complete printed counts, relevant algorithms and table data
were compared with frozen v3; the inherited verification archive is included
unchanged. The 1016 admissible fields themselves were freshly counted.
The historical archive and the prior review-assessment package distinguish
earlier computation from the new v4 work. No previous LLM's reported run is
silently relabeled as an independently executed v4 computation.
