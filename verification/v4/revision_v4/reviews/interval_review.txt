The elementary closed-interval argument is correct even for l=u and
l>u. For a nonempty forbidden interval, D above its lower endpoint
and avoidance imply D above its upper endpoint. If it is empty,

at least the next lower endpoint therefore propagates the strict bound.
The endpoint closures are justified by the strict original form bound.

therefore exclude every remaining finite D. Replacing A by one in
the upper endpoints changes only R_0 to 2C^2/(5B), as explicitly stated.
