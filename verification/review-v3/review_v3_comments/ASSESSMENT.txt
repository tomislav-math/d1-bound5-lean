==================================================

----------------

Conclusion
----------
The review identifies two real omissions in the written justification:
the f3 coefficient bounds and the large-AT justification in the cutoff
proof. Both admit short proofs under the existing hypotheses, with no
change to the main theorem or downstream constants. Several further
clarifications and selected simplifications are worthwhile. Wholesale
generalization of the approximation machinery is not recommended for this
revision. No counterexample or failure of the main theorem was found in
this focused assessment; this is not a new full audit of every argument
and every large finite enumeration.

Decisions on all numbered comments
----------------------------------

1. ACCEPT, WITH A SIMPLER PROOF. For w=y/x and rho=|w|<=1/4,
   16 P^3 |f3| = |1-w| |1-w^2|.
   The triangle and reverse triangle inequalities give
   (1-rho)(1-rho^2) <= 16 P^3 |f3| <= (1+rho)(1+rho^2).
   Substitution rho=1/4 gives exactly 45/64 and 85/64, hence the stated
   45/1024 and 85/1024. This avoids differentiation entirely. Add this
   proof where the bounds first occur; later parameter rows can cite it.
   The subscript 1000 in l_1000,u_1000 concerns AP, not a replacement of
   the hypothesis rho<=1/4 by rho<=1/1000.

2. ACCEPT. In actual Lemma 7.4 (label sm:bs7-cutoff), use
   AT>=T>=10^72>11. The bound from the proof of lem:general-three gives
   |F3| <= 1331/(4000 T^3) < 1/(3 T^3).
   AT>=9 alone would give 729/2048>1/3 and is insufficient. No subsequent
   cutoff changes are necessary.

3. ACCEPT. Use the discriminant cutoff 60 consistently, and explicitly
   say that 90/118/16 are totals over all listed fields. Cutoffs 60 and 64
   give the same 21 fields. Fresh exact enumeration gave 90/118/16 and
   zero final passes.

4. ACCEPT AS ALGEBRAIC CLARITY. The direct substitution gives
   (18/17) A^4 B^2. The printed A^3 B^2 is a valid weakening because
   A>=1, but an unexplained weakening is unnecessary.

5. ACCEPT. In actual Lemma 7.9 (sm:bs7-unit-pair), write
   u/v+sqrt(d)>2sqrt(d)>4, since d>=7. This supplies the explicit
   denominator estimate for the Legendre application.

6. ACCEPT. Move the allowance u=0 next to x-1=d u^2. Delete the final
   sentence that appears to attach the zero-square issue to a later
   modulo-4 argument. The current proof already covers the case.

7. OPTIONAL CONSISTENCY, NOT A MATHEMATICAL CORRECTION. In this branch
   the entries are explicitly positive rational integers, so t=T.
   If capitalization is changed, change both t<32 and t>48 to T.

8. ACCEPT THE AIM, CORRECT THE REFERENCE. Bennett's determinant identity
   is unnumbered, immediately after (2.4), in section 2, page 1721. It is
   not a numbered Bennett lemma. The nearby Lemma 3.4 is attributed to
   Rickert. Cite the actual location and explicitly give
   f_ij=P_ij(1/N) under nu=1/2 and Bennett's a_j=alpha_j.
   The equality follows termwise from
   (1+alpha_j/N)^(k-h_j) N^(-h_j)
      =N^(-k)(N+alpha_j)^(k-h_j).
   The extra pole factor already appears in Bennett's integral (2.1).
   Retain the existing denominator-clearing argument for complex points.
   Source: M. A. Bennett, Simultaneous rational approximation to binomial
   functions, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 348 (1996), 1717-1738,
   https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~bennett/B-Binom.pdf .

9. ACCEPT, WITH PRECISE SCOPE. Replace the three larger uniform constants
   in section 6: 735 -> 243, 166000 -> 165763, 26859063 -> 3113013.
   The other two are already 4143 and 2265. These are the least integers
   above the displayed rational bounds, not a claim of globally optimal
   approximation constants. Do not replace the smaller pair-specific or
   region-specific constants used in sections 8-9 by these uniform values.
   All five coefficient-sum families were freshly rebuilt, and all fifteen
   affected section 6 overlap comparisons pass. None decreases.

10. ACCEPT THE REPRODUCIBILITY POINT. Small exact margins are not errors.
   Margins below one for M are expected when taking ceilings of rational
   bounds. Keep exact arithmetic, source hashes, assertions and saved
   outputs. No arbitrary inflation of constants is needed.

11. RECOMMEND A BETTER ELEMENTARY ALTERNATIVE. Expansion of the order-four
   Vandermonde determinant into its 24 monomials gives
   |Delta_4|<=24 B S^2 T^3: sorting exponents 0,1,2,3 maximizes each
   monomial at the ordered moduli A<=B<=S<=T.
   This removes the maximum-modulus/Hadamard passage and gives direct-four
   constants 13 and 49/50; the zero-case constants 10 and 17 stay valid.
   With the already available C>A^2 T^5/S^2 and T>2ABS, the retained-gap
   proof still gives its ORIGINAL D>A^2 C^7/(S^2 T^4). The other use in
   the small-pair cutoff also retains its original final bound.
   Thus all N0 values, search bounds and interval tables may remain as
   they are. This proposal requires synchronized edits at three places:
   lem:direct-four, the end of lem:retained-gap, and the later direct-four
   application in sm:bs7-cutoff. Detailed derivations and exact checks are
   in the accompanying f3 assessment. Two reviewers checked the proposal.

12. OPTIONAL LOCAL STRENGTHENING, STRONGER THAN SUGGESTED. The existing
   weighted row sums give ||E||<11/96. Lemma 2.2 already gives D>6C and
   D>18 in this case. Put s=59/96,h=155/96. The same proof then gives
   |det N| < h s^2 (13/12+s/324) D^2
           = (18212679025/27518828544) D^2 < (2/3)D^2,
   and hence D>(3/2)ABC. This is a clean local improvement of 3/8.
   It is not needed to prove the main theorem. If adopted, keep existing
   downstream weaker bounds rather than launch a global optimization.

13. ACCEPT A SMALL DEDUPLICATION. State the sign-selection and factorization
   argument once and refer to it in the finite-form section. Make all
   nonzero denominator hypotheses explicit. Both existing proofs are
   correct; there is no need for a large new lemma hierarchy.

14. DEFER THE FULL GENERALIZATION. A common residue identity can be
   explained briefly, but a general pole-order theorem would add parameters
   and require separate integrality, clearing-factor and determinant
   statements. The finite and infinite constructions have different jobs.
   Keep their specialized verified statements; remove only genuine local
   duplication. A generalized result is not needed for this revision.

15. ACCEPT SELECTIVE CONSOLIDATION. Use one common endpoint template for
   the approximation rows, with local parameter substitutions. The existing
   section 8.1 overlap table is already largely reusable and even its
   weaker first two entries suffice for section 6. Retain the distinct
   fixed-triple cutoff comparisons in section 9: their purpose and
   substitutions differ. Avoid replacing concrete certificates by a long
   abstract lemma that hides the numerical checks.

16. KEEP THE SHORT SELF-CONTAINED PROOF. A citation is permissible but not
   necessary. The current Legendre proof is short and elementary and fits
   the author's preferences. The proposed Hardy-Wright theorem number was
   not needed and has not been independently verified in this round.

17. ACCEPT TARGETED NOTATION CLEANUP. Use omega,tau_K,nu_K,Delta_K for the
   integral basis and discriminant throughout. Plain tau would collide
   with existing numerical parameters, including 59/20. Give the five
   determinant-table constants another name, e.g. c_det. Preserve locally
   clear Vandermonde notation and avoid renaming every local h,H,F,Q,k.
   A short conventions table for recurring global symbols is useful.
   Recheck every norm, multiplication, conjugation and square-test formula
   after the basis notation changes.

18. ACCEPT. Assemble a versioned computational supplement with one runner,
   exact dependencies, source hash, script-to-output map and recorded
   expected results. Distinguish author code from any independent reviewer
   code. Replace 'complete output tables' by the more accurate 'tables of
   counts and cutoff constants' unless the complete data are also supplied.
   Add a permanent public URL only once the files are actually deposited.
   No journal-specific mandatory-code policy has been established here.

19. ACCEPT A COMPACT ROADMAP. Replace redundant introductory prose with
   three rows: zero small entries -> section 6/Proposition 6.1; one ->
   sections 8-9/Proposition 9.1; at least two -> section 7/Proposition 7.1.
   In sections 8-9 distinguish the 57 triples from the 40 tabulated pairs
   (one is excluded, leaving 56) from the additional (1,3,8) branch. Do not
   merge these counts inadvertently. A short paragraph is enough there.

Additional findings from the follow-up review
---------------------------------------------
* Add the useful bounded-graph explanation: x and -x are adjacent because
  1-x^2=(1-x)(1+x) is a square. The computed equality E=V/2 then shows
  that these are all edges of the computed graphs. This explains the zero
  triangle count; it does not replace the edge calculation or prove an
  unbounded graph classification. The preserved 1016 per-field records
  were inspected; the huge computations were not rerun in this round.
* The follow-up's statement that alpha and beta sum to 9999/10000 is not
  literally correct. The article correctly uses the weighted upper bounds
  (141/500)(59/20)+21/125=9999/10000. This is an imprecision in the review,
  not a correction needed in the article.
* Several section 7 lemma numbers in the review are shifted. Apply edits
  by LaTeX labels, not by the review's numbers.
