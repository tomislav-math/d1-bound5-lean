# Mathematical scope of the final Pell matching and assembly unit

## Preserved manuscript strategy
The immutable v4 unit-pair argument is unchanged. Every possible ordered D(1) sextuple containing -1 and 1 supplies a common squarefree parameter d with 7 <= d < 10000 and d congruent to 7 modulo 8. The real extensions have both signs of d*v^2+1, with u^2-d*v^2=2 and v nonzero. The compiled continued-fraction certificates enumerate the needed magnitudes up to the article's closed radius 10^550, using numerator cutoff 10^275.

Each pair is checked for both relative sign choices: x*y+1 is not an integer square and x*y-1 is not d times an integer square. Opposite signed entries of the same magnitude are not identified or discarded. These 15 working modules have passed pinned compilation, all 1392 declaration types/transitive-axiom checks, and 15 installed SAME-kernel replays.
