The definitions are polynomial over an arbitrary commutative ring;
none of the statements requires an entry, chosen square root, or determinant
to be nonzero. They cover zero squares and arbitrary finite dimensions.

Mathematical derivation: write t = u*r*v, where u^2=a, v^2=b,
r^2=ab+1, and e=t-ab. Then
e^2+2ab*e = t^2-(ab)^2 = ab.
For a finite root matrix M, the scaled matrix
N_ij=u_i M_ij u_j has
det N=(prod_i a_i)det M, provided u_i^2=a_i.
Define F_ij=N_ij-a_i a_j; then N=aa^T+F,
F_ii=0, and F_ij obeys the polynomial identity off diagonal.
For complex ab!=0, F_ij-1/2=-F_ij^2/(2ab).
